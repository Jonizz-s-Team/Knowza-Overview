## Summary

**Knowza LMS** handles everything at the institutional level:
- Multi-role dashboards (Admin, Teacher, Student)
- Classroom, group, and subject management
- Server-authoritative exam engine + Sentinel anti-cheat
- Gamification (XP, Streaks, Stars, Leagues)
- B2B SaaS subscriptions and billing

**Knowza AI** handles the personal learning intelligence layer:
- Conversational AI tutor with intent routing
- AI test generation and study planning
- Long-term user memory across sessions
- Agentic article generation with web search
- KnowzaShield prompt injection firewall
- B2C AI quota micro-transactions
